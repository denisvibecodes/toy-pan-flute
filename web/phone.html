Pan flute, phone

Hold the phone flat in front of you, the screen up, its bottom edge, where the microphone is, just under your lower lip. Blow across the bottom edge: the breath reaches the microphone held flat. If the note bends with no tilt, press Rest beside the bend's gauge as you hold the phone. Tilt the far end down to cover the pipe and bend the note; wobble the phone left and right for vibrato, which is cleanest with the phone flat. The keys are on the right, for the right hand.

The page asks for the microphone; it hears only your breath and nothing leaves the phone. Headphones keep the flute out of the microphone.

Settings

Breath: the microphone

The level of your breath's noise in the microphone, dB, after the gain beside the breath's gauge. Hold each for a second, then press its Set: the floor in silence, pp blowing gently, mf at your usual breath, ff hard. Under the floor there is no breath. The gain then moves all four together.

The breath is smoothed: it rises toward a stronger breath within the rise time and falls toward a weaker one over the fall time. A longer fall evens out a choppy breath; a longer rise softens the attacks and delays them.

Bend: tilting down

The angle of the phone's long axis below level, degrees. Hold the phone as you play with the pipe open and press Set on rest (or Rest beside the bend's gauge); tilt it down to the whole tone and press Set on the whole tone, which keeps how far below the rest it is. The semitone is held a third of the way. Tilting up from the rest bends nothing. The angle is low-passed before it bends: the vibrato's wobble leaks into it, and a cutoff under the wobble's 2.5 to 8 Hz keeps it out. Lower is steadier, higher follows a quick bend sooner.

Vibrato: the left-right wobble

The phone's sideways tilt, g, band-passed at 2.5 to 8 Hz, a hand vibrato's rates: a held lean, a slow sway and the hand's tremor bend nothing; only the wobble is heard. A compressor keeps a wide wobble near the ceiling: it turns the depth down within a fifth of a second and gives it back over three seconds.

Keys and sound

Latency

The phone beeps five times and listens for each beep in the microphone: the time is the speaker's and the microphone's delays together, as your breath has them, plus a few milliseconds of air. Stay quiet while it measures. Wired headphones only: Bluetooth adds a fifth of a second.
